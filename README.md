## Moin, I'm Lukas 👋

Head of Engineering for Cloud Platform & AI at [PlentyONE](https://www.plentyone.com) (formerly plentysystems AG), a B2B e-commerce SaaS company. My scope has covered five teams and 30 people at peak across platform, product and AI, reporting to the CTO. My teams also delivered the marketplace integration platform and the public Partner API in four months with a team of eight. Before that, as Director of Operations reporting to the CEO, I co-designed the product and engineering organisation with the founder. I've hired 20+ engineers and grown five into leaders.

GitHub shows the hands-on side of my work. For the leadership side (teams, organisational design, budgets), see [woehrl.net](https://www.woehrl.net).

My rule of thumb: **complexity has to earn its place**, in architecture and in organisations.

### 🛠️ What my teams run

- A Kubernetes platform behind 10,000+ customer systems, across multiple clusters, availability zones and regions
- GitOps with Argo CD and ring-based rollouts that stop automatically on error spikes; company-wide, we ship ~100 production releases per working day
- Security and compliance: SOC 2, TÜV and Amazon audits, penetration testing and vulnerability management
- The company-wide cloud budget: 40% lower monthly compute cost since 2023, while product usage grew
- AI coding agents with shared skills and governance, rolled out company-wide and used daily by 93% of engineers over three months
